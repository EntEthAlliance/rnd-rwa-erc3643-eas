Who signs, who decides, who enforces?

The passport describes the investor. The on-chain credential identifies John by an identity address linked to his wallet.

The provider signs the facts

A verification provider checks the investor's file and issues a signed record through the Ethereum Attestation Service (EAS). The supporting documents stay off-chain.

The issuer sets the rules

The issuer selects required checks and trusted providers for each one. In the example, both issuers accept John's identity check. Only the private credit issuer asks for accreditation.

Shibui checks. The token enforces.

At transfer time, Shibui checks whether current, trusted records satisfy every required rule. The token uses that answer alongside its own controls, such as a freeze or a holding limit.

How ten fields support eight eligibility checks

Six fields describe eligibility. One of them, accreditationType, supports three checks. The other four fields identify the investor and record the expiry, evidence hash and verification method.

Which eight checks can an issuer require?
01 · Identity (KYC)
The provider verified the investor's identity.
02 · AML
The provider recorded a clear AML result.
03 · Country
The recorded country passes the issuer's allow-list or block-list.
07 · Accreditation
The classification is in the set the issuer accepts.
09 · Professional investor
The classification is 1 or higher on Shibui's scale.
10 · Institutional investor
The classification is 4 (institutional).
13 · Sanctions
The provider recorded a clear sanctions result.
14 · Source of funds
The provider verified the source of funds.

The numbers are Shibui topic IDs. Issuers must map these model categories to their own eligibility requirements.

Who can sign or withdraw a credential?

An approved authorizer identifies which topics a provider may attest to. The issuer's operator adds that provider to its trusted list. An onboarding file, screening result or accreditation letter can supply the evidence for a provider's decision.

A provider can issue a new attestation when facts change or revoke an earlier one. Expiry or revocation removes that record from future checks. Another current record from a trusted provider may still satisfy the rule. Existing holdings stay in place.

What runs on Ethereum today?

The EEA reference implementation links a wallet to its investor identity. Its modified ERC-3643 registry calls isVerified(wallet) and receives a yes or no. Each verifier uses its configured rules and registered attestations on that chain.

An issuer on another chain needs a local attestation and its own trust configuration. The project documents a separate vLEI integration for evidence about organizations and authorized representatives.
